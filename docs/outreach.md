# Designer outreach — templates

For seeding the first 50 designers on OFFCUT. Use sparingly, personalize aggressively. Cold templates that aren't personalized get deleted.

---

## 1. Cold IG DM (short, primary channel)

> Hey [first name] — saw the [specific piece/recent post] and had to reach out.
>
> Just launched OFFCUT (joinoffcut.com), a commission platform for independent streetwear designers. Buyers brief a piece, you pitch it, you make it. 85% to you. Escrow on day one. No inventory.
>
> Inviting the first 50 designers in — zero platform fees on your first 3 commissions. 5-min apply: **joinoffcut.com/apply**
>
> No pressure, just thought your work fits. — Tristan

**Tweaks:**
- Always reference one specific piece. "Saw your work" is dead on arrival. "Saw the deconstructed Carhartt jacket from last week" lands.
- Drop the second paragraph if their account is small (<3k); skip the "first 50" framing — feels corporate at that scale.
- Sign with your real name, never "the team."

---

## 2. Cold email (when you have an email, not a DM)

**Subject:** Commissioning streetwear, properly — would love your take

**Body:**

> Hey [first name],
>
> I'm Tristan. I built OFFCUT (joinoffcut.com) because I'm tired of two things: malls selling me the same five hoodies, and watching incredible independent designers stuck waiting for IG DMs that never come.
>
> The pitch: buyers write a real brief (fabric, fit, references, budget), matched designers pitch back, buyer picks one, escrow holds the deposit, designer makes the piece. One-of-one. You keep 85%, no inventory risk, no platform fees on your first 3 commissions.
>
> I came across your work via [where] — [specific reference, e.g. "the panelled crewneck in your last drop"] — and the way you handle [specific technique/material] is exactly the lane OFFCUT is built for.
>
> If it sounds interesting, the application takes 5 minutes: joinoffcut.com/apply
>
> Either way, would love your take on what we're building.
>
> — Tristan
> joinoffcut.com

## Targeting checklist — who to DM

A good candidate has:
- ✅ A consistent visual identity / recognizable hand
- ✅ Posts that show finished pieces, not just moodboards
- ✅ Some indication they take requests (replies to "is this for sale?" comments, link in bio to a shop or DMs-open)
- ✅ Independent, not employed at a brand
- ✅ Makes the kind of thing that COULD be commissioned in 2–8 weeks (custom pieces, rework, small-batch, denim, knitwear, graphic tees, cut-and-sew)

Skip:
- ❌ Reseller / archive accounts (they don't make)
- ❌ Established brands with inventory and team (they don't need us)
- ❌ Pure conceptual / runway accounts (commission model doesn't fit)
- ❌ Accounts that haven't posted in 3+ months (probably done)

Communities to lurk before DM'ing:
- r/streetwearstartup (Reddit)
- Are.na (search "garment construction", "custom streetwear")
- CGM (Coverstitch Garment Makers) Discord
- Reach.fashion
- Niche TikTok #sewtok / #customclothing

## Tracking

Keep a simple spreadsheet — anything more is overkill:

| Handle | DM'd date | Reply | Applied | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

After 30 DMs and zero apps, the message is broken. After 30 and 3 apps (~10%), you're on track.
